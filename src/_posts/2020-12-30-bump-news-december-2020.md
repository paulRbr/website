Another month, another changelog, the last of 2020 this time. It's been a crazy year at Bump (in the good sense), and we are more than ready to take off like a rocket in 2021, but before that below are our latest product updates.

## Product

We continue to improve our support capabilities on current specifications, OpenAPI and AsyncAPI (news coming soon on that matter). We are working toward giving more autonomy to our users in order to create the best experience possible for something that can be a real hassle, API management.

### Custom domain

Maintaining a consistent experience is important when it comes to documentation, the ability to set up a custom domain is part of this consistency, such as easily finding your way on a map. It has been available from the very beginning at Bump, but sometimes, a feature needs time to mature and that's why you needed to email us to set it all up. We are glad to announce that you can now configure your custom domain directly from the app, by yourself and with ease.

![Bump custom domains](https://storage.googleapis.com/bump-blog-resources/changelog/custom-domain.png)

[Find here](https://docs.bump.sh/help/customization-options/custom-domains/) some more informations about how to proceed, but wait **that's not it**.

### Pricing plan

Custom domains are **now free** for everyone, which means you can setup custom domains on free plans, too! Our price plans are alive and they are part of our quest for making the best possible API management platform. We are always working to add value without necessarily moving the pricing cursor.
